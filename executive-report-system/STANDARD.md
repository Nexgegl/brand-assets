# NEXGEGL Executive Reporting Standard v1.0

Status: RATIFICATION CANDIDATE
Scope: Internal executive, board, investment, product, technology, vendor, partnership and governance decision reports.

## 1. Purpose
Convert analysis into governed executive decisions using the canonical sequence:

EVIDENCE → COUNCIL (when triggered) → KFSA → ECONOMICS → RISK → GOVERNANCE → GATES → DECISION.

The objective is not more analysis. The objective is better decisions, faster execution, lower risk and measurable impact.

## 2. Evidence discipline
Every material statement must be classified as one of:
- DOC / VERIFIED FROM SOURCE
- CLAIM
- ASSUMPTION
- INFERENCE
- MISSING
- EXT-VERIFY

A source statement is not independently verified merely because it appears in a document.

### Mandatory ownership rule
ABSENCE OF EVIDENCE ≠ EVIDENCE OF COUNTERPARTY OWNERSHIP.

If ownership, rights, authority, exclusivity, liability, IP or data rights are not stated, classify them as UNDEFINED / MISSING. Never infer that one party owns an asset merely because another party's rights are not documented.

## 3. Decision states
Use only when supported:
- KILL — stop.
- FIX — continue after a defined deficiency is corrected.
- SCALE — evidence supports controlled expansion.
- ALERT — material risk or unresolved issue requiring attention.
- BLOCKED — mandatory evidence, authority, access, approval or control is missing.

Do not force KILL/FIX/SCALE when BLOCKED is the accurate state.

## 4. Council escalation
Council is an escalation mechanism, not the default mode.

Trigger it for material, high-uncertainty or high-impact decisions, or when explicitly requested.

Council opinions are analysis, not evidence. Majority agreement does not establish fact. The Chairman must synthesize disagreements and may conclude BLOCKED — EVIDENCE REQUIRED.

## 5. Mandatory report architecture
1. Cover
2. Executive Decision
3. Situation / Opportunity at a Glance
4. Thesis
5. Counter-Thesis
6. Council Findings (if triggered)
7. Economics
8. Risk Map / Red Team
9. Governance & Decision Rights
10. NEXGEGL Strategic Role / Options
11. Conditions Precedent
12. Execution Path / Gates
13. Final Decision
14. Evidence & Source Notes

Sections may be compressed for shorter reports, but Executive Decision, Evidence, Economics, Risk, Governance, Gates and Final Decision may not be omitted for material decisions.

## 6. Executive Decision page
Must answer:
- VERDICT
- Executive judgment
- Capital / resource decision
- Strategic participation
- Rationale
- Conditions
- Next Best Action
- Owner
- Evidence required
- Pass gate
- Stop condition
- Reversibility

## 7. Economics
Never manufacture financial inputs.

Separate:
KNOWN INPUTS / ASSUMPTIONS / CALCULATIONS / CONCLUSION.

If required inputs are absent, state:
INVESTMENT ECONOMICS: NOT COMPUTABLE FROM AVAILABLE EVIDENCE.

Where relevant request: revenue, contribution margin, capex, opex, cash requirement, unit economics, break-even, payback, ROI/IRR, sensitivity, downside and opportunity cost.

## 8. Risk and Red Team
Do not fabricate probability.

Classify risk severity by decision impact:
CRITICAL / MATERIAL / MANAGEABLE.

For consequential decisions, run a pre-mortem:
"Assume this failed. What are the five most plausible causes, and which are visible today?"

## 9. Governance
For material recommendations define where applicable:
Decision Owner / Approver / Executor / Evidence / Authority / Control / Exception Process / Audit Trail / Acceptance Gate.

AI recommendations are advisory unless an approved governance mechanism explicitly grants authority.

## 10. Execution gates
Every material recommendation should define:
OWNER / DELIVERABLE / DEPENDENCY / DEADLINE / EVIDENCE / PASS CRITERIA / STOP CONDITION.

Completed ≠ Accepted. Acceptance requires evidence.

## 11. Internal vs external outputs
### Internal IC Memo
May include full Red Team, assumptions, conflicts, counter-thesis, peer-review disagreement and sensitive decision logic.

### External Executive Decision Brief
Target 5–7 pages unless otherwise requested. Do not expose internal deliberation, speculative counterparty motives, sensitive assumptions or confidential Red Team reasoning. Preserve the decision, evidence basis, agreed actions, controls and next steps.

## 12. Production test
Before finalizing:
- Is the recommendation supported by evidence?
- What assumption could reverse it?
- What would fail in production?
- What is the financial exposure?
- Who owns the action?
- How is success measured?
- What governance gap remains?
- Is there a simpler/reversible path?
- What is the next decision?

If a critical answer is unknown, expose the gap.
