# NEXGEGL Brand Voice — Draft Control Standard

**Status:** PENDING_APPROVAL for editorial tone examples; canonical constraints remain in force.  
**Scope:** NEXGEGL corporate brand; product-specific language requires its own approved standard.

## Voice baseline
- Institutional, plainspoken, precise, evidence-led; explain business impact before architecture.
- Arabic: Modern Standard Arabic, clear professional phrasing appropriate for Saudi enterprise buyers; do not use inflated superlatives.
- English: concise enterprise language; distinguish implemented capabilities, prototypes, proposals, and roadmap.
- Do not claim that product, security, certifications, revenue outcomes, customers, or integrations are verified without an authoritative source.

## Terminology controls
- Use **NEXGEGL** exactly in uppercase.
- Position as **Decision Governance Layer** / **طبقة حوكمة قرار**.
- Governance Runtime is the runtime governance authority; KFSA is the internal decision engine. Do not attribute formal KFSA verdicts to content-generating agents.
- KILL / FIX / SCALE are formal verdict types only when sourced from KFSA; ALERT is a pre-decision gate.
- Do not position NEXGEGL or its products as an “Operating System” or “OS”; avoid unapproved product names.
- Never imply the layer replaces a client's ERP, CRM, or existing operating systems.
- No fabricated client logos, paid deployments, certifications, savings, numerical improvements, testimonials, or regulatory approvals.

| «نضمن خفض التكاليف 40%» | «نساعد على تنظيم مسارات القرار وتوثيق أدلتها؛ يُقاس الأثر في نطاق تنفيذ محدد.» |
| «المنصة تحصل على اعتماد الجهات السعودية تلقائيًا» | «تُراجع متطلبات الامتثال وفق نطاق العميل والأدلة المتاحة.» |
| “Our AI decides which companies must stop spending.” | “Governed decision workflows require verified evidence and the authorized decision engine.” |
| “Fully integrated with every ERP.” | “Integration capabilities are assessed per connector and deployment scope.” |

## Client interaction
State the customer's operational problem, decision owner, evidence required, implementation boundary, and next action. Distinguish commercial proposals from deployed functionality. No urgency manufactured without a real deadline.

## Publication gate
Every factual claim needs a named, dated evidence reference and claim owner. Product copy needs product-owner approval; brand-level changes need brand-governance approval. Unknown content must be labelled **PENDING_APPROVAL**, not silently approved.

## BB-03 review boundary (proposed)
Arabic and English examples are review fixtures, not approved copy. Test forbidden terminology and exaggeration on content proposals; a green keyword check cannot establish appropriate institutional tone. A human brand reviewer must assess context, readability and localization before approval.
